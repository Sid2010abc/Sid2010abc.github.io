A black hole's size (event‑horizon radius) is fixed entirely by its mass: r₊ = GM/c²·(1+√(1−a²)). Its average density then falls as 1/M² — supermassive holes can be less dense than air. You can't set size and density independently; that's real physics, not a simulator limit.

Physics notes: orbits integrate real Newtonian N‑body gravity (symplectic Euler).

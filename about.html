About

Building things that move, sense, and learn as I do the same.

I'm a junior at Huntley High School headed toward a computer engineering major, with a focus on AI, robotics, and the future of computers. I like problems that live at the boundary of hardware and software, Where a line of code has to survive contact with the forces of the universe. On my own, I explored AI, then apply it to Robotics, and am now looking to make computers better through quantum computing.

Manipulation Python

Humanoid Robot

An upper torso with AI-controlled arms (3 DOF each) and a head fitted with a camera, microphone, and speaker, aimed at handling natural, spoken human requests. Designed to respond as a friend and colleague as I pursue future ventures, he is currently addressed by his working title, Bob (Basic Operational Biomorph). He is almost entirely software at this point but will soon have a physical body to control. See the build log for updates starting October 2026 upon the release of this website.

PCB Design KiCad

Custom PCB Design

Designed a custom printed circuit board in KiCad, translating a circuit from individual components and connections into a manufacturable hardware system. The project expanded my interest in understanding how complex systems emerge from carefully designed interactions between simple components.

Autonomous VEX V5

VEX V5 Competition Robot

Programmed autonomous and driver-controlled behavior using GPS, vision, bumper, and color sensors for the state robotics team, plus mechanical design and iterative prototyping. Helped earn the Think Award for documented engineering design and analysis. However, we now face problems with finding a head coach and funding for the 2026 to 2027 school year. Despite dozens of emails and meeting with the school board, we do not expect to be allowed to continue. We stay optimistic but also accept that we were never a top priority at our school.

Computer Hardware Raspberry Pi

Raspberry Pi Cyberdeck

Built a portable Raspberry Pi cyberdeck by integrating computing hardware, power, input, and display systems into a single functional device. Used it during demonstrations for students at Leggee Elementary, turning a personal hardware project into an opportunity to share how computers can be built and understood from the inside out. Used it to explain everything from CLIs to Qubits because of its immense attention grabbing potential.

Community Demonstration - 2025
Fabrication Workshop Design

DIY Soldering Fume Extractor

Designed and built a factory-inspired fume extractor for my soldering station, approaching a workshop problem as a system of airflow, filtration, structure, and usability. The project reinforced how engineering principles can turn an ordinary workspace problem into something worth designing from scratch.

Practice Project - 2026
Electronics TTP223

Touch-Controlled Soldering Station Light

Built a touch-controlled light using TTP223 capacitive touch sensors for my soldering station. The project doubled as hands-on soldering practice while introducing me to the relationship between sensors, signals, and physical outputs in an electronic system.

Practice Project - 2026
Circuit Analysis Multisim

Circuit Simulation and Analysis

Explored series, parallel, and combination circuits through Multisim, connecting circuit theory with simulation and measurable behavior. These experiments helped establish the habit that now drives many of my projects: model a system, test its behavior, and revise my understanding based on the results.

Foundation Project - 2026
Repair Reverse Engineering

Sky Viper Drone Repair

Diagnosed and repaired my brother's old Sky Viper drone, tracing problems through an unfamiliar system rather than starting with instructions or a blueprint. The repair strengthened my interest in learning how complex technology works by taking it apart, understanding its components, and bringing the system back to life.

Restoration Project - Throughout 2020 Pandemic
Early Engineering Experimentation

The First Things I Built

Before I knew what engineering was, I built a cardboard boat, cardboard car, and miniature wind turbine simply to see whether they would work. Those early experiments were imperfect, but they established the same curiosity behind my projects today: understand an idea by turning it into something real, testing it, and figuring out why it succeeds or fails.

Single-qubit gates
H creates superposition · X flips |0⟩↔|1⟩ · Z flips the phase of |1⟩ · S adds a quarter-turn of phase (doesn't change probabilities, only shows up as color).
Two-qubit gate — CNOT
controltarget
Flips the target qubit only when the control qubit is |1⟩. This is what links two qubits into an entangled state.
Three-qubit gate — Toffoli
c1c2tgt
Flips the target only when BOTH controls are |1⟩ — a quantum AND gate.
